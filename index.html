How to play

  1. ▰ ↑
    1. Pick a parcel

    The cardboard boxes in the tray are yours to load. Touch any box.

  2. ▦
    2. Drag it into the truck

    Drop on a green outline. You can also tap a box, then tap where its barcode sticker belongs.

  3. ↻
    3. Turn it to fit

    Tap Rotate to turn your box. Use Tray to take a loaded box back out.

Fit every parcel to send the truck on its way. Bolted cargo stays fixed in later deliveries. Pack around it. Stuck? Undo or Hint can help.

Keyboard on the board: 1–6 selects a box, arrow keys move, Enter places, and R rotates. Tap the driver or press H for the horn. Tap the front of the cab or press L for headlights.
